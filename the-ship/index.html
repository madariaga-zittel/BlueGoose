Brooklyn-class light cruiser

U.S.S. Honolulu (CL-48)

Known to her sailors as “The Blue Goose.”

The ship

Built for speed, firepower, and the Pacific war

Commissioned in 1938, Honolulu served throughout the Pacific and survived bomb and torpedo damage severe enough to send her home for major repairs more than once. Her wartime service earned eight battle stars.

She was present at Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941, later fought in the Solomon Islands, supported amphibious campaigns, and served during the return to the Philippines.

Wartime survival

Damage, repair, and return to action

Pearl Harbor

A Japanese bomb near miss distorted the hull and caused flooding while Honolulu was at Pearl Harbor.

Kolombangara

A torpedo struck the cruiser during the July 1943 night battle, requiring extensive repairs.

Leyte

Another torpedo hit during the 1944 Philippines campaign ultimately sent Honolulu back to the United States.
