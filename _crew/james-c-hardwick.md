James C. Hardwick is identified as MM1 aboard the U.S.S. Honolulu. Another archive photograph records his first meeting with Elva E. Pegel at the ship’s decommissioning dance at the Ben Franklin Hotel in Philadelphia on March 4, 1946; the description notes that they later became husband and wife.
