James Ervin “Snowie” Zittel served aboard the U.S.S. Honolulu (CL-48) during World War II. His service, memories, and connection to the ship inspired the creation of this memorial.

This page is dedicated to preserving his story alongside the stories of the officers, sailors, and Marines who served aboard the ship affectionately known as **the Blue Goose**.
