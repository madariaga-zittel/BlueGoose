John “Hotshot” Ferguson Jr. served as a welder aboard the U.S.S. Honolulu from the Aleutians through Leyte Gulf. The supplied description says he helped keep the ship afloat after it lost more than 70 feet of its bow, also worked in the boiler room, and reloaded anti-aircraft guns.
